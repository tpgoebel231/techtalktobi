About

Tobias P. Goebel

Content and advisory on autonomous driving systems (robotaxis, driver assistance), AI, and robotics.

My path started where language meets code. I studied computational linguistics, computer science, and general linguistics in Bonn and Edinburgh, graduated in 2003, and then spent two decades in enterprise software. In 2010 I moved from Germany to the US.

Along the way I went from engineering and consulting to technical product marketing and found my niche in conversational AI and the contact center world, with keynotes, industry panels, and a lot of writing. One highlight: a CogX Award for “Best Consumer Chatbot” for an AI wine sommelier I built for Lidl in the UK.

After five years in IoT, I went fully independent in 2025. I have been hooked on the future of mobility for years, especially the engineering and the regulation behind self-driving cars. That is how TeslaTobi started: my German YouTube channel with deep dives into autonomous driving, robotics, and AI, mostly around Tesla and its toughest competitors.

With TechTalkTobi I am going international. It is my English-first platform with a wider lens: what AI, automation, and the other big innovations really do and what they change, explained so that everyone gets it, not just the nerds.
