Tracker2026-08

Waymo Rollout

Tracking Waymo's autonomous vehicle deployment and market expansion: cities, service mode, and what 'driverless' actually covers.

  1. 2020 · Public robotaxi

    Phoenix

    The original commercial ODD. Still the reference city for weather-easy, mapped L4.

  2. 2024 · Public robotaxi

    San Francisco

    Density, hills, and politics. The city that taught everyone else about edge cases and city hall.

  3. 2024 · Public robotaxi

    Los Angeles

    Scale play. Freeways plus sprawl: a different ODD than the grid of SF.

  4. 2025 · Service expanding

    Austin

    Overlaps the Tesla robotaxi conversation geographically. Useful for side-by-side watching.

  5. 2025 · Service expanding

    Atlanta

    Airport and highway mix. A test of whether the playbook survives humidity and Southern sprawl.

  6. 2026 · Announced / staged

    Further metros

    The 2026 question is cadence: how many new ODDs per year before the map-and-ops cost dominates.

Waymo is the existence proof that a geofenced, heavily mapped, multi-sensor L4 service can carry passengers without a person in the seat. The question for 2026 is not 'does it work in Phoenix'. It is how expensive each new city is, how weather and density punish the ODD, and how fast the playbook copies.
