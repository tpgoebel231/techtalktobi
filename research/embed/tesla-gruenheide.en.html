Core Team Profile

Assembly-line work and manual logistics disappear entirely. The remaining team consists mainly of fleet engineers, AI supervisors, mechatronics technicians for robot maintenance and legally required inspection roles.

Fact Check: Media Reports Status: verified July/fall 2026

Is Tesla Optimus really already training at Gigafactory Berlin?

Yes: According to internal letters (reported by Golem and trade media, among others, in July 2026), teams in Grünheide were equipped with camera backpacks to record work steps in high resolution for Optimus AI training. In parallel, pre-production units (Gen 2/3) are piloting simple logistics and cell-handling tasks in the 4680 battery building. That makes Grünheide an active data hub for Optimus Fleet Learning.
